## The item

`notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`, M-03 (a). The prefill stager copies a step's expert slabs into a
ring at the top of the activation arena, and it arms only for a step whose arena level leaves that region
unlent: `min_rows = top_level + 1` — 1025 tokens when the largest level below the full plan is 1024 rows
(`core/engine_bringup.cpp:5086-5110`, `core/engine.cpp:2490-2513`). The scheduler's busy-chunk floor was
`stage_rows_ / Q * Q`, that same number rounded **down** to the quantum: 1024 at Q = 64. A chunk held back
to exactly the floor, in a step where the rows budgeted for the work behind it do not materialise — the
reserve is a forecast, `core/sched/scheduler.cpp:2700-2712` — is a step of 1024 rows, so it takes the
1024-row level, the stager is handed no ring, and the chunk reads the pooled experts in place. That is the
off-by-one. Part (b) of the item, a ring right above the 1024 level and arming from ~128 rows, is a
separate card; this is (a) alone.

## The change

- `core/sched/scheduler.cpp` / `core/sched/scheduler.h`: new `Scheduler::stage_floor(quantum)`, the
  stager's smallest pass rounded **up** to the quantum, one quantum when there is no stager. `busy_cap()`
  uses it wherever it used the floor: the initial `max(budget - reserve, floor)`, the split test that
  leaves the rest of a checkpoint interval for one step, and the final `max(cap, Q)` clamp. `min_rows`
  itself is untouched — it is the engine's fact about the arena, not the scheduler's to move.
- Numbers at Q = 64: 1025 → 1088 (at Q = 16, the item's own 1040). A 2048-row interval with a 272-row
  reserve: before, the split rule held the chunk at 1024 and left 1024, so either half could take the
  lending level on its own; after, the interval cannot hold two armed chunks (1088 + 1088 > 2048), so the
  chunk goes out whole at 1776 in one armed step and 272 rows (< Q) are the remainder — one unstaged pass
  per interval instead of two, and the one that stages is the one that reads the whole pooled set.
- `tests/sched_test.cpp`, `TEST(busy_cap_rounds_the_stager_step_up_to_the_quantum)`: the floor rounds up at
  Q = 64 and Q = 16, a held-back chunk of a 2048-row budget whose reserve is not used, the split case (the
  chunk alone is a step the stager arms for, or no split), and the same over a sweep of reserves. Red
  without the change (`stage_floor(64)` = 1024 vs 1088, `busy_cap(0, 100000, 2048, 1500)` = 1024 vs 1088),
  green with it.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURES.md`, `docs/GUIDE.md`: the rows that printed the floor — the `--busy-prefill-chunk`
  operator number against the smallest pass the stager arms for, and the `auto` table.

What it does not do: it gives no ring to the 1024 level (b), so a prompt whose last chunk is 129-1024 rows
still reads the pooled set in place. That is the item's headline gain and it stays open with (b); (a) only
recovers the held-back chunks.
