## F-17 f32 instead of f64 in `idx_pool_gated`'s norm / trig

Backlog §4 F-17. Built, tested, and **off by default**: `hyv4flash` reads
`RADIANCE_HY4_IDX_POOL_F32` (`0`, the default, to `3`) and puts the value on every pool op as the
optional int attribute `norm_f32` (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_indexer.h`). The measurement below is
the price of turning it on; nothing in the tree asks for it.

The item asks for the mean square's sum and the ring's trig in f32 because RDNA4 issues f64 at 1/16
rate. Those are two independent halves, so the switch has two bits and the note gives both:

| `norm_f32` | what runs in f32 |
|---|---|
| 0 | nothing: libref's f64 form, the default |
| 1 | `mean(y^2)`'s 128-term sum (`r4d_idx_pool_gated_f32.hip:144-148`) |
| 2 | the rotary's `theta^(-2i/rotary_dim)`, `cos` and `sin` (`:180-184`) |
| 3 | both |

libref keeps the f64 form and is unchanged in every arm: it is the law. The gate's `exp` stays f64
in every form — the item names the norm and the trig, and `exp` is neither (see "seen and left
alone").

### The bound, and how the kernel's output was checked

`r4d_selftest --case idx_pool_gated_f32` (`libr4d/r4d_selftest.cpp`, case `idx_pool_f32_case`) runs
the row against libref's row on two position ranges x two rotary modes x the three values. The
first groups of a sequence, and positions to 62277 — with `rotary_dim` 64 and `theta` 1e7 the
fastest pair's argument is ~6e4 rad there, which is where f32 trig is at its worst. The bound is on
the bf16 output and the bf16 cache: at most 2 % of elements move, `rel_l2` at most 1e-3, the largest
absolute move at most 2e-2 of the buffer's rms; and where a move is expected (`norm_f32` 2 or 3, long
positions) some element must move, so a switch the row ignored cannot pass.

| `norm_f32` | near positions | positions to 62k |
|---|---|---|
| 1 sum | 0 of 4736 differ, `rel_l2` 0 | 0 of 4736 differ, `rel_l2` 0 |
| 2 trig | 0 of 4736 differ, `rel_l2` 0 | 45 of 4736 (0.95 %, max 35 ulp), `rel_l2` 3.3e-04, max\|d\|/rms 7.7e-03; imrope 28 of 4736 (0.59 %), `rel_l2` 2.5e-04 |
| 3 both | 0 of 4736 differ, `rel_l2` 0 | the same 45 / 28 as the trig alone |

Why that tolerance is right for the dtype: the key is rounded to bf16 either way, so one bf16 step is
2^-8 = 3.9e-3 relative and a *single* flipped rounding is already 3.9e-3 on that element; `rel_l2`
1e-3 over the buffer therefore means "at most a few per cent of elements moved by one step", and
2e-2 on the largest move against the rms covers the small elements that move by a whole step. The
f32 sum's own error is 128 x 2^-24 = 7.6e-6 relative (halved by the `rsqrt`) and moves nothing at
all; the trig's phase error is `|ang| x 2^-24`, which is under half a bf16 step at these angles and
moves about 1 % of the keys there.

`rad-avx-check`'s hand cases (`libavx/avx_check_hand.cpp`) hold the CPU row to libref at the same
`norm_f32` values, `rel_l2` 1e-4 for the sum alone and 1e-3 for the trig: measured 1.8e-05.

### keptn's KL on u3

`radiance --config` in KL mode (`docs/TOOLS.md`), TP2, `hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3.rad`, no spec, the lane's
own placement (`scripts/backlog/nospec.yaml` rendered by `lane-cfg.py`), the whole vocabulary per
position. Corpus `scripts/backlog/prompts.py`: `long_prompt(22000, 7)`, `long_prompt(24000, 13)`,
`CODE_PROMPTS[3:9]` joined — 3 documents, 15398 scored positions, so the positions run to ~8.4k and
the trig's argument to ~8.4e3 rad.

The reference is **this build's own default arm** (f64, `kld-record`) over the same tokens, recorded
once and scored three times. That is what isolates the switch: the two arms differ in nothing else,
and scoring the default against that reference gives KL 0.000000 and top-1 100.000 % exactly, and so
does scoring `norm_f32=1` against a `norm_f32=1` reference, so the pipeline is bit-deterministic and
the numbers below are the switch and not run-to-run noise. (keptn's `kl-stats.md` numbers are the
*u3 against the bf16 HF model* gap over its own 160-prompt, 40443-token corpus; that corpus is not
in the tree and not in this lane, so this note cannot reproduce them, and its greedy-answer tokens
sit at PPL 1.98 where KL is small. These documents sit at PPL 112.86, and KL grows with how flat the
reference distribution is, so compare the per-position shape, not just the means.)

| `norm_f32` | asks, 390 pos | pf6, 6646 pos | src2, 8362 pos |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 sum | 0.000 / 100.00 % | 0.116 / 95.28 % | 0.343 / 88.83 % |
| 2 trig | 0.025 / 96.15 % | 0.196 / 91.80 % | 0.389 / 87.67 % |
| 3 both | 0.025 / 96.15 % | 0.191 / 92.04 % | 0.402 / 87.77 % |

The shape of that is the whole finding. The sum's error is 500 times under a bf16 step and moves
*no* element of the synthetic case, yet it is not free: the indexer selects blocks from scores over
those keys, and a key that crosses a rounding boundary moves a near-tie selection — so what costs KL
is not the key's error but the selections it flips, and the flips grow with the number of blocks a
position chooses among (0 at 390 positions, 5 % of top-1 at 6.6k, 11 % at 8.4k). The short document
is untouched by the sum alone, and the trig at 390 positions is already 4 % of top-1 because its f32
`pow` differs from the first token on. A tenth of long-context positions change their answer
materially; the median position does not move.

served.

### Verdict

F-17 as written is a bad trade: −4.4 us a launch at decode and −0.05 ms a step (0.4 %, unmeasurable
end to end) for mean KL 0.30 and 10 % of long-context top-1 at 8.4k positions. The half that gets
most of the speed (the norm's sum, 3.6 of the 4.4 us) is not the cheap half either — it is the
half whose error is smallest in the keys and still costs mean KL 0.24 through selection flips.

It stays in the tree off by default, behind `RADIANCE_HY4_IDX_POOL_F32`, because that is what the
item asks for and because the measurement above is what the next lossy lever on this op needs to
compare against: **this op's arithmetic is judged by KL on long positions, never by the keys'
rel_l2.** Splitting it does not rescue it: the sum carries 3.6 of the 4.4 us and 0.236 of the 0.301
mean KL, the trig the rest of both.

### Seen and left alone

- The gate's `exp` in `r4d_idx_pool_gated_f32.hip` stays f64: the item names the norm and the trig,
  so the switch does not touch it, and its 1/16 rate is paid in both arms.
- libavx's `cos` and `sin` were already f32 (`avx_hy4.cpp`), so there bit 2 is the `pow` alone; the
  note's KL numbers are the HIP row's, which is what serves.
- The trig's f32 loss could be taken down to ~1 ulp by reducing the angle mod 2*pi in f64 before a
  f32 `cos`/`sin`. That is a different lever (one f64 op a element, not 16) and is not measured
  here.
- `tests/museglimmer_test.cpp` does not compile in this tree (`-Wc++11-narrowing`, `int64_t` to
  `unsigned int` at :200, :453, :466, :467), `avx_check`'s `embed_lookup_q` case fails on this
  container's refused io_uring (`docs/DOCKER.md:108`), and `arch_test` fails 18 cases at the base
  commit `71bab241` itself (qwen35_w8a8 / qwen35moe_w8a8 step counts) with this change checked out
  and without it. All three are older than this change and none touches the pool.
